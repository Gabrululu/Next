# Next — Brand

Everything needed to write, design and post about Next consistently. All values below are the ones used in the product (`web/src/app/globals.css`, `web/src/app/layout.tsx`).

---

## 1. Core

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Next (always capitalised; written "Next." with the violet full stop in the wordmark only) |
| **Tagline** | **Everyone in line. Nobody cuts in.** |
| **One-liner** | A fair way to hand out things that run out. |
| **Short description** | People join any time while it's open, a fair draw picks the winners, and anyone can check it. |
| **Category** | Fair access for scarce things: seats, spots, scholarships, drops |

### Promise
Joining first doesn't matter. No bots racing, no refresh button, no "sold out in 3 seconds". Everyone who shows up during the window has the same chance, and the result can be checked by anyone.

### Why it exists
Every scarce thing (a workshop seat, a concert ticket, a scholarship) ends up going to the fastest clicker or the best script. That rewards bots and people with fast connections, not the people who actually want it. Next replaces the race with a fair, open draw.

### Personality
- **Calm:** there's no rush, and the copy never creates urgency.
- **Honest:** we say what it does and what it doesn't do.
- **Plain:** a first-time visitor understands every sentence.
- **Playful but precise:** a hand-written logo, an exact system.

## 2. Voice and vocabulary

Write as if explaining it to a friend who has never used crypto.

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| join, get in line | register, call `register()` |
| spots | slots |
| the draw, a fair draw | seed block, Fisher-Yates, block hash |
| a random number nobody knew in advance | public seed, entropy, VRF |
| anyone can check it | verifiable on-chain, recompute the keccak |
| your pass (can't be transferred) | soulbound ERC-721, token |
| account | address, EOA, wallet (unless the person brought their own) |
| network fee (covered by the app) | gas, MON, sponsorship |
| sign in with email | connect wallet, EIP-1193 |

Rules:
- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Talk to the reader ("you"), not about "users".
- Never promise "one per person". Say **one entry per account**.
- Never invent numbers, partners or users. The only numbers we show come from the chain or from the recorded test run, labelled as simulated.
- Technical words belong in `ARCHITECTURE.md` and the verifier, not on the landing page.

**Examples from the product**
- Hero: "Everyone in line. Nobody cuts in."
- Sub: "For seats, scholarships and anything else that runs out. Joining first doesn't matter. A fair draw picks the winners, and anyone can check it."
- Status: "Registered." · "Waiting for the draw." · "You won." · "Not this time."
- Email opt-in: "Email me the result. One email after the draw. Nothing else."

## 3. Visual identity

### 3.1 Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `ink` | `#0a090c` | Page background (near-black) |
| `ink-2` | `#131118` | Raised panels |
| `ink-3` | `#1d1a24` | Controls, empty dots |
| `line` | `#2a2633` | Borders, dividers |
| `paper` | `#f5f3f7` | Primary text |
| `mute` | `#a39fae` | Secondary text (7.6:1 on ink) |
| **`volt`** | **`#9d6bff`** | **Accent: electric violet.** The picked dot, primary buttons, the full stop |
| `volt-2` | `#c3a6ff` | Accent text on dark |
| `ok` | `#5ee6a8` | Success (valid pass, confirmed) |
| `bad` | `#ff6b81` | Errors (not valid, failed) |

Dark-first. Violet is rare on purpose: it marks what was **picked** or what you should **press**. Never use it as a large background fill. A soft radial glow at the top of a dark surface is the one exception.

### 3.2 Typography (all Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License)

| Role | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Logo / wordmark | **Sedgwick Ave Display** | Hand-lettered, rotated −3°. Only for "Next." and the favicon "N". |
| Headlines | **Bricolage Grotesque** 700–800 | Large, tight leading, slightly negative tracking. |
| Body / UI | **Geist** | Regular text and controls. |
| Numbers / technical | **Geist Mono** | Block numbers, addresses, the verifier. Tabular numbers for counters. |

### 3.3 Logo

- **Wordmark:** "Next" in Sedgwick Ave Display, paper colour, rotated −3°, followed by a **violet full stop**.
- **Mark / favicon:** a hand-lettered "N" on an `ink` rounded square (corner radius ≈ 22%), with a violet dot at the lower right.
- **Clear space:** at least the height of the full stop on every side.
- **Don't:** recolour the letters violet, remove the rotation, add effects, or set the wordmark in another font.

Files (generated by `web/scripts/gen-brand-assets.tsx`, run with `pnpm brand:assets` in `/web`):
- `web/src/app/favicon.ico` (16/32/48 px)
- `web/src/app/icon.png` (192 px), `web/src/app/apple-icon.png` (180 px)
- `web/src/app/opengraph-image.png` (1200×630, the share card)

### 3.4 The motif: the line of dots

The product in one picture: a row of identical grey dots (everyone in line) where a few light up violet with a soft glow (the picked ones). Use it for headers, dividers, loading states, social images and stickers. Order never matters, so the picked dots are spread out, never at the front.

### 3.5 Shape and texture

- Large radii: cards 28–32 px, buttons 16 px, pills fully rounded. Squircles where possible.
- Tactile surfaces: subtle inner highlights and soft shadows, like physical controls (dial, fader).
- Panels framed like a small app window (three dots in the corner) for "live" or "recorded" content.

### 3.6 Motion

- Slow and smooth (0.5–1.6 s ease-out). Nothing flashes or shakes.
- Signature moments: words rising in the headline, dots filling the line, the reshuffle when the draw happens.
- Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: everything must make sense with no motion.

### 3.7 Illustrations and honesty labels

Anything that isn't real data is labelled: "Illustration", "Example". Recorded test runs say "simulated entrants". Live data says where it comes from ("via Envio", block numbers).

### Bios
- **X / Farcaster (160):** Everyone in line. Nobody cuts in. Fair draws for seats, scholarships and drops. Join any time, a fair draw picks, anyone can check it. On Monad.
- **One sentence:** Next replaces the race for scarce things with a fair draw anyone can check.

### Post templates
- *Launch:* "Sold out in 3 seconds? Not here. With Next, everyone who joins while it's open has the same chance. A fair draw picks the winners, and you can check it yourself. → next-monad.vercel.app"
- *For organizers:* "Running a workshop with 30 seats and 300 people? Open a window on Next, share the link, and let a fair draw decide. No bots, no refresh wars."
- *Proof:* "We fired 200 sign-ups at the same second on Monad testnet (simulated entrants). 200/200 confirmed in under 5 seconds, and the draw checked out. Numbers: [link to README]."
- *At the door:* "Winners show a QR signed by their account. The organizer scans it, done."

### Visual do's and don'ts
- ✅ Dark background, one violet highlight, big plain headline, the dot line.
- ✅ Real screenshots of the app.
- ❌ Crypto clichés (coins, rockets, chains), stock photos of crowds, fake numbers.
- ❌ Words like "bot-proof", "unique humans", "guaranteed fair to each person": the honest claim is *one entry per account* and *anyone can check the draw*.
